How LQX AFRICA Works

A structured, secure, and transparent process connecting eligible commercial opportunities with appropriate liquidity and capital providers across Africa.

OUR PROCESS

From Opportunity to Execution

LQX AFRICA provides a structured process for assessing, verifying, structuring and connecting eligible commercial opportunities with appropriate liquidity and capital providers.

1

Submit

A supplier, contractor, business, project sponsor or other commercial participant submits a commercial opportunity or liquidity requirement.

2

Qualify

LQX conducts an initial assessment to determine whether the opportunity appears commercially credible and falls within the company's operating parameters.

3

Verify

Relevant parties, companies, documents, contracts, purchase orders, invoices, counterparties and supporting information are subjected to appropriate verification.

4

Assess

The transaction is assessed from a commercial and risk perspective, including documentation, counterparties, payment cycles, repayment sources and structural considerations.

5

Structure

Where appropriate, LQX works with relevant parties and professional or financial partners to determine what type of liquidity or capital structure may fit the transaction.

6

Match

An eligible opportunity may then be introduced or matched with an appropriate liquidity or capital provider.

7

Execute

The actual financing, investment, credit or other regulated transaction is undertaken by the appropriate provider under its own approval, documentation, risk and regulatory requirements.

8

Monitor / Close

Where appropriate and contractually agreed, LQX may monitor transaction milestones, documentation, reporting and completion.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can engage with LQX AFRICA?

Suppliers, contractors, buyers, project sponsors, capital providers, banks, DFIs, insurance companies, corporate organizations, development partners and strategic stakeholders may engage with LQX AFRICA, subject to applicable eligibility and verification requirements.

How do I submit a commercial opportunity?

Eligible commercial participants can submit an opportunity or liquidity requirement through the LQX Liquidity Desk. Each submission is subject to initial qualification, verification, due diligence and applicable provider requirements.

Does LQX AFRICA provide the financing directly?

LQX AFRICA operates as commercial liquidity infrastructure and an intermediary platform. Where appropriate, eligible opportunities may be introduced or matched with suitable liquidity or capital providers. Any financing, investment, credit or other regulated transaction is undertaken by the appropriate provider under its own requirements.

Is every submitted opportunity funded?

No. Submission does not guarantee qualification, funding, investment, approval or transaction completion. Opportunities remain subject to eligibility, verification, due diligence, risk assessment, provider requirements and applicable regulatory frameworks.

Is LQX AFRICA available across Africa?

LQX AFRICA is being developed as commercial liquidity infrastructure with an Africa-wide orientation. Availability, participation and transaction structures may vary by market, provider requirements and applicable regulatory frameworks.

What happens after an opportunity is matched?

The appropriate liquidity or capital provider conducts its own assessment, approval, documentation, risk review and regulatory processes. Where appropriate and contractually agreed, LQX may monitor relevant transaction milestones, reporting and completion.

Have a Commercial Opportunity or Liquidity Requirement?

Submit an eligible commercial opportunity or liquidity requirement for initial consideration through the LQX Liquidity Desk.
