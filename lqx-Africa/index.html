HOW IT WORKS

Eight Stages of the LQX Process

LQX uses a structured process to move from commercial opportunity submission toward appropriate transaction pathways.

1

Submit

Submit a commercial opportunity or liquidity requirement through the LQX Liquidity Desk.

2

Qualify

The submitted opportunity is reviewed against applicable qualification criteria.

3

Verify

Applicable KYC, KYB, transaction verification and due-diligence processes are undertaken.

4

Assess

Commercial characteristics, counterparties, documentation and liquidity requirements are assessed.

5

Structure

Where appropriate, a potential transaction pathway may be considered and structured.

6

Match

Eligible opportunities may be considered for matching with appropriate liquidity or capital providers.

7

Execute

Transactions proceed between appropriate parties subject to their respective requirements, approvals and applicable laws.

8

Monitor / Close

Where appropriate and contractually agreed, LQX may monitor transaction milestones, documentation, reporting and completion.
