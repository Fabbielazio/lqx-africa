THE LQX LIQUIDITY DESK

CONNECTING COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITIES WITH APPROPRIATE LIQUIDITY & CAPITAL

The LQX Liquidity Desk is the central access point for businesses, suppliers, contractors, project sponsors and capital providers seeking structured commercial opportunities across Africa.

Through a structured process of submission, qualification, verification, assessment and matching, LQX helps connect eligible commercial opportunities with appropriate liquidity and capital providers.

THE LQX PROCESS

FROM COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY TO APPROPRIATE LIQUIDITY

Every submission enters a structured process designed to establish the nature, quality, eligibility and appropriate pathway for the commercial opportunity.

01

Submit

Provide the core details of your commercial requirement, opportunity or capital mandate.

02

Qualify

LQX reviews the submission against applicable commercial and eligibility criteria.

03

Verify

Relevant business, transaction and counterparty information is subjected to applicable verification, KYC/KYB and due-diligence requirements.

04

Assess

The opportunity and its underlying commercial characteristics are assessed to determine an appropriate pathway.

05

Structure

Where appropriate, the commercial requirement may be considered for a suitable transaction or liquidity structure.

06

Match

Eligible opportunities may be considered for matching with appropriate capital or liquidity providers.

07

Execute

Subject to provider requirements, approvals, documentation and applicable legal and regulatory requirements, appropriate parties proceed with execution.

08

Monitor / Close

Where appropriate and contractually agreed, LQX may monitor transaction milestones, documentation, reporting and completion.

LIQUIDITY DESK FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the LQX Liquidity Desk?

The LQX Liquidity Desk is a structured access point through which commercial opportunities, liquidity requirements and capital-provider interests can be submitted, assessed and considered for appropriate pathways.

Does submitting a request guarantee funding?

No. Submission does not constitute approval, funding or a commitment by LQX or any capital provider. Any potential transaction remains subject to eligibility, verification, due diligence, risk assessment, structuring, provider requirements and applicable laws and regulations.

What type of commercial opportunities can be submitted?

Depending on applicable eligibility criteria, submissions may include genuine commercial transactions involving contracts, purchase orders, invoices, supply agreements, project agreements and other commercial arrangements.

What documents should I provide?

Relevant supporting information may include contracts, LPOs, invoices, project agreements, company documentation and other materials that help establish the commercial opportunity and its counterparties.

Can capital providers use the Liquidity Desk?

Yes. Capital providers and institutions can register their interest, mandate, preferred markets, sectors, transaction types and indicative capital parameters. Any subsequent engagement remains subject to applicable requirements and processes.

Does LQX provide regulated financial services?

LQX is positioned as commercial liquidity exchange infrastructure and an intermediary connecting eligible commercial opportunities with appropriate liquidity and capital providers. Regulated financial services, where required, are subject to applicable authorization and appropriately licensed parties.
